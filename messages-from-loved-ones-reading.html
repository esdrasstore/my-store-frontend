What this session actually involves

This is a gentle, unhurried session for connecting with someone you've lost — a parent, partner, friend, or anyone whose absence you're still carrying. There's no script to follow. Some people come with a specific question or something left unsaid; others simply want to feel close to their person again for a little while. Both are completely welcome.

Your advisor will let you set the pace. If it helps to talk beforehand about who you're hoping to connect with, that's fine. If you'd rather begin quietly and see what comes, that's fine too. There is never any obligation to share more than feels comfortable.

Who this is for

Anyone missing someone who has passed, whether the loss is recent or long carried. People hoping for a specific message or a sense of closure. People who simply want to feel close to someone again, without a particular question in mind. There's no right amount of time that needs to have passed, and no wrong reason to book this.
