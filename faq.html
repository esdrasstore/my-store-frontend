Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about preparing for, scheduling, and engaging in your private session with Venicit. Clarity begins before we even speak.

Absolutely. Discretion and privacy form the absolute foundation of our practice. Every detail shared prior to, during, and after your session remains entirely between you and your guide.

We do not share client identities, retain sensitive personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for your booking, or discuss your private matters with any third parties.

Expect a focused, grounded, and entirely personalized conversation. There is no performance, no spectacle, and no predetermined script. We meet you exactly where you are.

Whether you have booked an intuitive reading, a tarot spread, or a strategic reflection session, our aim is to cut through the noise, examine the energies or patterns at play, and leave you with actionable clarity.

We recommend finding a quiet, private space where you will not be interrupted. Take five minutes before our scheduled time to center yourself and reflect on the primary question or situation you wish to address.

You do not need to prepare notes or a rigid timeline of events; simply come with an open mind and a willingness to explore the truth of your situation.

To preserve the sanctity, privacy, and flow of the conversation, we ask that you do not digitally record the session. You are, however, warmly encouraged to take handwritten notes during our time together.

For certain services, such as Tarot Readings, high-resolution visual maps of your spread will be provided to you digitally following the session for your records.

All sessions are conducted strictly by appointment. You can request a time by navigating to our Sessions page and selecting the service that aligns with your current needs.

Due to the high-touch nature of our work, availability is limited. If you require same-day availability, please look for the "Most Requested" premium session which accommodates urgent timelines.

We understand that life occasionally requires a shift in plans. We kindly request a minimum of 48 hours' notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment.

Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled time are non-refundable, out of respect for the time reserved exclusively for you.
